## 🔎 What the Dashboard Explores

### 🕒 Website Traffic Patterns
The dashboard includes a session-by-hour view and a weekly traffic pattern to help examine when website activity occurs.

### 📈 Conversion Performance
The dashboard presents conversion rates by hour, supporting comparisons across different time periods.

### 🌐 Acquisition Channels
The channel effectiveness visual compares sessions and conversions across Organic Search, Direct, Referral, Social Media, and Paid Ads.

### 🌍 Geographic Distribution
A country-level visual presents the distribution of sessions across the locations included in the dashboard.

### 📱 Customer Segments
The dashboard includes a device and user-segment comparison across desktop, mobile, and tablet.

---

## 💡 Opportunities for Further Analysis

- **Traffic optimization:** Investigate hourly and weekly traffic patterns to identify periods worth examining further.
- **Conversion improvement:** Compare traffic and conversion performance across hours and acquisition channels.
- **Channel evaluation:** Assess which acquisition channels contribute most to sessions and conversions.
- **Customer experience:** Examine device-level engagement to identify potential mobile or desktop usability opportunities.
- **Market analysis:** Compare geographic performance across the countries represented in the dashboard.

*These are proposed analytical opportunities, not claims of improvements already achieved.*

## 🎯 Project Takeaway

BrightCart demonstrates how a retail performance dashboard can bring website activity, conversion measures, acquisition channels, geographic distribution, and customer segments together to support structured analysis and business decision-making.

**Focus Areas:** E-Commerce Analytics · Website Traffic · Conversion Analysis · Customer Segmentation · Data Visualization
